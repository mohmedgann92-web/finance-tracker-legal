Common questions

How do I delete my account?

Settings › Account › Delete account. The app first shows what will happen: your personal space and spaces where you are the only member are deleted; a shared space you own is handed to its longest-standing member; your entries in other people's spaces stay with that space's owner. Your cards, bank accounts and bill payments are deleted. You can back up first. It needs your password and an internet connection.

How do I get my data out?

Transactions › Export or import › Export all transactions (CSV). Settings › Backup saves the whole space as one file, optionally protected with a passphrase. We cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

How do I restore a backup?

Settings › Backup › Restore from a backup. A restore always creates a new space; it never overwrites one.

How do I turn AI insights on or off?

Settings › Preferences › AI insights (only the owner of a space can change it). "Include notes" under it decides whether short note snippets are shared. Cards, bank accounts, bill payments and statements are never sent. See the privacy policy for exactly what is sent.

How do I sign in with Face ID or Touch ID?

iPhone only. Sign in once with "Remember me on this device" ticked, then go to Settings › Account › Face ID / Touch ID and switch on "Sign in with Face ID / Touch ID". Next time, tap the Face ID button on the sign-in screen. "Lock the app" in the same place asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode when the app opens and when you come back after 30 seconds away. If your face or finger is not recognised, your phone's passcode works.

I want my family to see our spending.

Create a shared space (Settings › Spaces) and invite them by email. Everyone in a shared space sees everything in it. Of your cards they see only the name and last 4 digits of the ones you used for entries in that space.

Does the app connect to my bank?

No. You type entries, paste a bank message, scan a receipt or speak them. You can also import a statement file you downloaded from your bank's website or app (CSV, Excel or PDF), or, on iPhone, photograph a paper statement. The file or photo is read on your phone and never uploaded; only the entries you tick are saved, and an import can be undone for 30 days. The app never connects to a bank or broker, never asks for a bank login and never moves money.

Is my card number stored?

No. A card or bank account is a label you type: a name, the bank, and the last 4 digits only. The app never asks for the full number, the expiry date or the security code. "Pay card bill" only records a payment you made at your bank; the app cannot pay anything.

How do I undo a statement import?

Transactions › Export or import › Import a bank or card statement. Under Recent imports, tap Undo next to the import. Only the person who imported it can undo it, within 30 days. Undo removes every entry and bill payment that import added, including ones edited since.

My PDF statement asks for a password.

Type the password your bank gave you. It is used to open that file on your phone and is never stored or sent; you will be asked again next time.

Known limitations

  • Voice entry, receipt scanning and paper-statement scanning are available on iPhone only.
  • Reading paper statements and scanned PDFs works with Latin script only, so Arabic-only statements cannot be read from photos. Text PDFs in Arabic, CSV and Excel files work.
  • Share and ETF prices need the household's own free Twelve Data or Alpha Vantage key (Investments tab). Coins, gold and silver are priced automatically.
  • Only new transactions can be added while offline; edits and settings need a connection.
  • Exchange rates and prices are public daily reference rates, not live quotes.
  • AI reports are informational and are not financial or investment advice.
